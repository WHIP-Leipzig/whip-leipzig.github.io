## Audience
The meetup is meant to be open to everyone who agrees with its [principles](/en/principles.html) and also lives them openly. The soft age limit for participants is around 40. The organizers reserve the right to filter the audience and, if needed, refer people to other meetups, so that a pleasant get-together and good experiences can be ensured.

## Thematic focus
The thematic focus of the meetup should always be BDSM. The organizers try to give every meetup a thematic focus to encourage exchange. Ultimately, the goal of the meetups is to contribute to educating interested people and to help them find a safe way into the topic.

## Frequency
The [WHIP meetup](/en/faq.html#what-kinds-of-meetups-are-there-at-whip) usually takes place once a month, if possible on the first Friday of the month. The [Play-WHIP](/en/faq.html#what-kinds-of-meetups-are-there-at-whip) is usually planned for the second Tuesday of the month.
